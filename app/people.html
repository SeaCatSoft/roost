What the two roles can do

Owner — everything a member can, plus inviting people, changing roles, and removing access.

Member — records checks and weighings, edits assumptions and prices, starts cycles, and can delete a batch permanently.

There is no view-only role. A member is full trust over the farm's records; the difference is only control over people.
