On hand, free, promised — what the columns mean

On hand — physically here: everything processed, less what has gone out, plus or minus any corrections.

Promised — already spoken for by confirmed orders that have not yet been fulfilled. Drafts are not counted; a draft holds nothing.

Free — on hand minus promised. This is what you can actually sell today.

Free goes negative when orders are taken ahead of processing. That is not an error — it is the amount the current cycle still has to deliver.
