On hand, still needed, to buy — what they mean

On hand — bags that should be in the store right now.

Still needed — bags the open cycle's feed plan says it will still open, of that ration, before it ends.

To buy — still needed, less on hand. Zero means you are covered for this cycle.

A count is the store at the end of its day: deliveries and bags opened that same day are treated as already in it. Count after the day's work is done.
